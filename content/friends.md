# Friends

> Published on Sep 15, 2026

Over the years, I've been fortunate enough to learn the value of phrases like *thank you, that was such a kind thing to say, you just made my day!, I love you,* and *I'm sorry.* They may not mean much on their own--especially when looked at in a vacuum, completely devoid of any context--but they are capable of doing a lot of heavy lifting given the proper prelude.

I was sitting around kicking it with some friends yesterday. In the early hours of the morning, as we were getting ready to leave, I let out a simple *thank you* to show my appreciation for (and to) a person I care for to the deepest degree. A large chunk of my desire to re-calibrate after years of seemingly remaining socially, emotionally and creatively dormant comes from conversations I've been having with this person, and in that sense, I recognize I owe a lot to them. 

Ever since going completely sXe, I've started putting more emphasis on doing things like this; it helps me remain grounded in myself and my community. This particular type of honesty is what I hold most dear in the world, and I've learned never to pass up the opportunity to let the people close to me know just how much I appreciate their presence.

They simply smiled and said, *No, thank you. I really appreciate our conversations.*

Sometimes I struggle with finding the fuel to get up in the morning. Work and life responsibilities often generate a sort of artificial reasoning for breaking the desire to rot in bed from now until forever-- but a couple of kind, honest words that you know came from a sincere place can help you recolor your world with an entirely new palette.

Friends make the world go round.
