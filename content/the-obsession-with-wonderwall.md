# the obsession with 'wonderwall'

> Published on Sep 16, 2026

I'm currently at my local cafe, doing some admin work + penning future musings. 'Wonderwall' by British rock darlings Oasis came on. The station it came on is called--you guessed it!--Rock radio. 

I never got the obsession with this song. Almost every new guitar trainee will opt for learning that song + Deep Purple's 'Smoke on the Water.' I sort of understand why--they're simple songs perfect for new blood. But, like, come on--to hear that stuff on the radio? This frequently? Can we, like, not?

Thank you. --IS
