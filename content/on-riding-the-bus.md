# on riding the bus

> Published on Nov 10, 2026

Being dependent on public transport is perhaps the only part of my existence I find excruciatingly painful. I don't posses the time management skills necessary to utilize it effectively, I don't like how crowded it gets sometimes, and I'm not a fan of its shallow, art-less liminality. The bus is not a device for enabling creativity, and thus I find it boring. 

If you're under the age of 18, you shouldn't be allowed to get on any form of public transportation. You'll just make the experience worst for everybody on board. 

November is a slow burn. 
--IS
