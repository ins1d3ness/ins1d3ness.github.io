# Original TMNT (Mirage Comics)

> Published on Sep 16, 2026

I've been re-reading Kevin Eastman and Peter Laird's Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles -- the original iteration of the four ninjutsu-practicing sewer-dwellers. I believe I did a proper read-through for the first time around 2010 or 2011.

I remember the original run being much darker than the 1987 or 2003 shows. Even though the 2003 series followed the comics more closely -- probably because of Peter Laird's direct involvement -- it was held back by the executives at 4Kids. Season 4 of that show was exceptionally dark, so it's not like they didn't try, but ultimately it was short-lived and they had to shelf Season 5 in favor of a kid-friendlier revamp.

Two episodes of Season 4 come to mind regarding the big suits interfering. One is Insane in the Membrane, where Baxter Stockman was subjected to some gory body horror imagery. He was also, quite literally, going insane in that episode, but I'd guess what ultimately pulled the plug were the visuals. The episode ended up being made -- I have no idea how it got through the 4Kids chain of command -- but there were some mishaps regarding TV scheduling and all that.

Another one is Nightmares Recycled -- an episode that didn't even get past the development stage. It had a cool storyline with a pretty big twist in the middle: in the episode, it would've been revealed that Hun and the Garbageman were originally conjoined twins separated at birth, complete with a gory visual package to boot. Peter Laird had a [blog entry](https://peterlairdstmntblog.blogspot.com/2012/01/blast-from-past-357-february-11-2005.html) about the production shenanigans that went on during that period, and his remarks weren't glossy, to say the least.

The 1987 show, in comparison, felt like a bad Larry David trip in audiovisual form. I choose to ignore its existence as hard as humanly possible, and so does Peter Laird. I think. I have no idea where Kevin Eastman stands regarding all that.

The original Mirage run is where it's at. It's dark, gritty, dirty, and grimy -- sometimes inappropriate, edgy, and over the top.

Absolutely Lovable. --IS
