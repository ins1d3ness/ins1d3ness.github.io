# FAUST mailing list

> Published on Nov 9, 2026

For the uninitiated--FAUST has a semi-active (well, these days not so much) mailing list. It's open to everyone and anyone that may posses a burning desire to discuss art, theory, life things--anything really!

If you'd like to post on the mailing list, do so by sending an email to *faustcollective@freelists.org*. 

You can sign up--using your email, of course--[here](https://www.freelists.org/list/faustcollective).
