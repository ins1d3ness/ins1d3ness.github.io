# Sickness: A Novel - project notes so far

> Published on Sep 14, 2026

I'm getting ready to leave my apartment for the night—figured I should jot this down quickly so I can get it off my to-do list. (Most--if not all--of my motivation or "drive" to do things essentially boils down to being annoyed by unfinished entries staring me down daily.)

*Sickness* has been in the works since late 2023 or early 2024, sometime around then. Kind of like *HARMONIA*, I owe this project's birth to a uni assignment. We had to do what's called snapshot writing for our creative writing class, and I chose to write what would later become the opening scene of the novel--the one where Mindy and John are at a party and John reminisces about everyone acting like a bunch of fucked-up junkies.

Yes, I was thinking of William S. Burroughs when I was writing that.

I'd like the book to come out in late 2027--that's the current idea. I still need to finalize the last chapter.

Right now, in terms of fiction, *HARMONIA* takes precedence. Given that it's still unfinished, the presence of *Sickness* is proving to be incredibly haunting. I'll keep you posted. --IS
