# Friends Again

> Published on Nov 10, 2026

Talking to friends about friendship is something I seem to be doing [a lot](https://slijepcevic.mataroa.blog/blog/friends/) these days. It's an activity I enjoy quite a bit, and that, on its own, is a fact about myself I find exceptionally weird. It is only recently that having these types of meta conversations became worthy of exploration for me. Normally, I'd stick to the pragmatic approach--where discussion is a technology for exploration, knowledge acquisition, and/or production.

Having recently realized I'd managed to hang onto what are now lifelong friends, my friendships became much more grounded in the idea of longevity. Togetherness was always the bedrock of everything, but this specific type of grounding allowed for different readings to happen outside of that ceiling (which is very finite).

Working on an essay to elaborate on this rn. TBR in the coming weeks.
