> [!info] Spoiler-free
> Everything in this directory is written so that you can browse it before you've read a word. Summaries stop at each story's premise.

**HARMONIA** is an original fiction universe, fifteen years in the making. It began as a school project and grew into a web of connected stories: school comedy, cosmic mystery, cyberpunk noir, grounded war drama, a farmhouse diary and a few detours into myth. Underneath all of it are the same two things — friendship, and the stubborn refusal to stop.

This page is the hub. Every story branches off from it, and every story links back.

## New here?
Start with [[HARMONIA Origins|Origins]]. It's the foundation, and everything else grows out of it. After that, follow whichever branch looks most interesting. Save [[HARMONIA Odyssey|Odyssey]] for last.
