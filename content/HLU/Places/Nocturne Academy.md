↩ [[HARMONIA]]


A school in [[Caldwell]], founded in the late 1800s as Harrow Preparatory and renamed in the 1940s.

Students know a few things about it without being able to say who told them: the lower level is sealed, there's a cold draft in one hallway that doesn't match any season, and the computer lab is [[Mr. Darrow]]'s kingdom. The gym is run by a PE teacher with the intensity of someone doing a job assigned by forces beyond the school board.

The building behaves like a character: patient, watchful and unbothered.
