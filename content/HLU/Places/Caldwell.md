↩ [[HARMONIA]]


The city where [[Nocturne Academy]] stands, and the home base of most of the HLU.

It looks different in each branch. In the school years, it's an ordinary city under strain, with a growing class divide. In [[City at War]], it becomes a battlefield. In [[HARMONIA 2099|2099]], it has grown into a vertical megacity owned by a corporation.
