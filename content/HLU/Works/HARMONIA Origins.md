## Summary
John has spent twenty-three years away from the city where he grew up. Then he sees his own double at a cemetery, and beside him a girl with a face he never expected to see again — and the pull is too strong to ignore. He goes home to Caldwell and starts tracking down his old classmates, one by one.

The reunion does not go well. Old friends, old teachers and old grudges turn out to be tangled up in something much bigger than any of them, and the past is not finished with them.

Funny, fast and self-deprecating on the surface, with a lot of grief underneath. Everything else in the HLU grows out of this story.
