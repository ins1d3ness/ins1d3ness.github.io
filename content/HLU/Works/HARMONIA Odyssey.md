## Summary
The last book. Where every other branch picks up a thread, Odyssey is the one that finally ties them all together. It follows each character individually, one chapter at a time, across a very long span.

It is a story about friendship, camaraderie and perseverance — and about what those things look like when life takes people in different directions.
