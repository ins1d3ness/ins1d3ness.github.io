## Summary
A just king has been given the power to know any truth. He rules justly precisely because he refuses to use it on one particular thing: a death that would tell him whether he is a good man or a monster. His closest friend, certain of the answer, is trying to force the door. The king would rather lose his friend, and perhaps his realm, than find out.

Full political tragedy, played straight. Few jokes, and what jokes there are have teeth.
