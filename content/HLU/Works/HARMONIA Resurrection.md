## Summary
The sequel to [[HARMONIA Origins|Origins]], and a deliberate change of register. Where the HLU has always been John's voice, this story is told from [[Lauren]]'s point of view, in the present tense.

It is a small, intimate book about one year, one person, and a stubborn refusal to give up on him. It asks what it costs to bring someone back — and whether everything that comes back is the same thing that was lost.
