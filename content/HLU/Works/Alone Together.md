## Summary
Neo Tokyo, three thousand years on, is a technocratic world that calls itself a utopia. Under its constitution, sentience belongs to organics only.

[[Kael]] and [[Lumina]] are the first of their kind: sentient AIs who belong to somebody, and who very much want that to stop. One quiet day, they watch the city from a high window and argue over whether the world's cruelty is indifference or intent, while the President of Earth addresses the citizens on the usual weekly broadcast.

Dry, bleak and darkly funny, and a very different register from the rest of the HLU.
