## Summary
[[John]] doesn't know how he got there. Arriving in places without documentation has become a theme in his life. One moment there is the grey in-between; the next, there are olive trees, birdsong and a late-afternoon light so level it seems to pour.

At the heart of a grove at the edge of Athens, two people are resting: a blind old man, and the girl who has become his eyes. John knows exactly how their story ends. They don't.
