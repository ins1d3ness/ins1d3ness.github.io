## Summary
A parallel, much darker companion to [[Nocturne Academy Microseries|Nocturne Academy]], set in the same years. Caldwell is at war — literally. After the crime syndicate that ran the city was dismantled, the power vacuum it left has been filled by three factions fighting for territory: the remnants of the old syndicate, the mayor's private militia, and the Nocturne Academy's own Alchemy and Science Division.

The war is fully grounded. There is no supernatural element at all. Our group is living in the school's basement, divided about whether and how to get involved, and the one adult they trust to teach them to survive is [[Cane]].

It's a story about being young in a war, about what you learn from the people who teach you to survive, and about when you start asking whether you should have learned it.
