## Summary
A diary, kept by a narrator who never names herself — the way you never introduce yourself to yourself on the page. The setting is a run-down farmhouse in Northampton, Massachusetts, belonging to her late grandparents. The year is 2017.

The group has come here to recover from something that happened at school. They don't talk about it. [[Vic]] gets the power and water running; [[John]] disappears into the forest for hours at a time; and everyone has the same nightmares at the same time, and nobody says so.

Quiet, funny and tender, with the weight of the unspoken.
