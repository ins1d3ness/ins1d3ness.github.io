## Summary
As a modern-day companion to [[The Girl at Colonus]], [[John]] finds himself in the walled garden of a care facility in the last hour of October light. A man who was once very public, and has since been brought low, sits there in a wheelchair, his face turned toward a sun he can't see. Beside him is his daughter Anna, who gave up everything to push the chair, and who notices John before he has decided whether to be noticed.

It's a quiet story, built from conversation and the weight of something that hasn't happened yet.
