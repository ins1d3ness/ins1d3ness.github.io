## Summary
Seven short stories about nine kids at a school that sits on top of something it shouldn't. The kids don't know — that's the entire engine.

Set in the mid-2010s, this is the last stretch of ordinary life the cast will ever get: friendship as it actually forms, which is to say badly, accidentally, through insults, detentions and one very long bus ride. It's warm, specific and genuinely funny. Anything strange stays at the edges of the frame — a draft that shouldn't be there, a teacher's odd phrasing, a gate that's always locked.
