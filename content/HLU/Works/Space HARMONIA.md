## Summary
The gang finds itself far from home, on **Centralia**, an interstellar port in System 8A: a meeting point for trade, business, cultural exchange and crime. It's a crossroads for several species — humanoid **Eights**, native to Systems 8A and 8B; energy beings called **Astraloids**, who need exosuits; and a sentient AI race.

System 8A is a democratic, plural society that also serves as an asylum for people fleeing System 8B, a xenophobic, fascist dictatorship. The tension between the two systems has already turned into a hot war — and our group lands in the middle of it with no time to get their bearings.
