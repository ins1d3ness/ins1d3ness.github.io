## Summary
Seventy-five years on, Caldwell has become a vertical megacity owned by **Nocturne Systems** — a corporation that grew out of the bones of an old prep school. The city is two cities stacked on top of each other: the **Crown**, the gleaming upper floors where the sun still reaches, and the **Sediment**, everything below the smog line, in permanent neon dusk.

Nocturne's product is the future itself, sold in slices. Nobody in the Sediment has ever seen the Director who runs it, and wherever his name should appear in a file, it is blacked out. They just call him **Dr. X**.

It's the same people, in a very different world: a memory archivist, a data-necromancer, a ripperdoc, a noodle-stall smuggler, a netrunner, a solo, an insider and a fixer. The tone is sardonic, noir and sharp — [[John]]'s thesis is that the old comics got the year exactly right and the hero completely wrong.
