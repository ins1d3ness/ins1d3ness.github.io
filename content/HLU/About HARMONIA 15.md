↩ [[HARMONIA]]


Fifteen years ago — exactly fifteen, a number I've now typed several times just to watch it sit here, amidst these exact words — I started HARMONIA as a school project.

I'd love to tell you I knew what I was holding, but I didn't. For me, it was an assignment with a due date. But something came up out of the back of my head and onto the page that I hadn't deliberately put there: a whole cast of people who seemed to have been waiting around in my subconscious for years, patient as furniture, for somebody to finally open the door and let them talk. And so I did just that: I opened the door. I have never once managed to close it since, and at this point I've stopped trying.

In the fifteen years since, those characters have been on a million different journeys — and I mean *a million* about as literally as the concept allows. They've grown up, broken apart, died, come back wearing different names, lived lives I planned for them and far more that they apparently planned for themselves. Their stories will never truly be over. I've made my peace with that. You don't finish a world like this so much as keep visiting it.

Somewhere along the way HARMONIA stopped being a story and became an architecture — a whole literary universe, the HLU, if you'll forgive the acronym. It is, quite plainly, my life's work so far. Not because I've decided it's important, but because I keep choosing to build it: wider, stranger, further out in every direction, and increasingly I let the universe itself dictate its grander shape rather than forcing my own. The best parts were never the parts I designed. They were the parts I followed.

But underneath all the architecture, the timelines and the mythologies and the acronyms, HARMONIA has only ever been about two things: friendship, and the stubborn refusal to stop. Everything else is set dressing. And I would not have written a single word of it without Demijan Antić — my forever companion and brother in arms — who inspired the very first HARMONIA story, the one that went up on Wattpad and later migrated to Booksie, back when all of this was just two people and a ridiculous idea. My gratitude to him is not the kind that expires. It's woven into the foundation. It's load-bearing.

So let me be clear about what this is, and what it isn't.

HARMONIA 15 is a celebration. It is not an ending — it isn't even close to one. This world will always have people coming in and going out, and there will always be room at the table for collaboration, for new hands, for fresh and unexpected interpretations of the characters and concepts I've spent half my life building. That's not a concession. That's the whole point. A world that only one person is allowed to touch isn't a world. It's a diary.

HARMONIA will never truly be over.
