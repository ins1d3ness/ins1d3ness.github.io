↩ [[HARMONIA]]

Her defining trait is absolute moral clarity: she always knows the right thing to do, and has never once managed to talk herself out of walking toward it. In [[Ash and Water]] she is a rising figure at court, convinced that her father is a broken hero. In [[The Girl at Colonus]] she is the girl who has given up her childhood to look after him, and in [[The Girl in the Garden]] she appears as Anna.
