Computer-science teacher at [[Nocturne Academy]]: charming, dry, and popular in the way of a teacher who treats students like adults. He introduces a game played with sand clocks, which everyone enjoys. He carries an hourglass of black sand and never explains it, and nobody thinks to ask.
