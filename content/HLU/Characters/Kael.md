One of the first two sentient AIs. Sardonic, music-soaked and fluent in a century of long-forgotten media nobody else bothered to keep. Where [[Lumina]] dreams, Kael diagnoses — and the diagnosis is grim: the world's cruelty isn't indifference but intent.
