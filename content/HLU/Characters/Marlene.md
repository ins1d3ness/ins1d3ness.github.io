↩ [[HARMONIA]]

The one who always knows a guy. A gift for logistics, an ability to get almost anyone to agree to almost anything, and a private inner world she doesn't show off. Where [[Nicole]] is ambition, Marlene is sideways intelligence — and she has a habit of dragging everyone into proximity with things they should have stayed away from.
