↩ [[HARMONIA]]

Says almost nothing. When he does speak, it reframes everything. Dirty hair, unbothered, and the engineering backbone of the group: if something needs rebuilding, he has already started.

He collects soda cans and can't throw them away, and nobody — including Vic — has ever managed to explain why.
