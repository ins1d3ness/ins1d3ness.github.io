↩ [[HARMONIA]]

The first person to talk to [[John]] at Nocturne Academy, for no reason except that it seemed like the right thing to do. Warm, sardonic and generous, he's the social center of the class without ever appearing to want the job. John would follow him into a fire without checking whether there was a plan.

He turns up in almost every branch of the HLU, and never quite as the same man twice.
