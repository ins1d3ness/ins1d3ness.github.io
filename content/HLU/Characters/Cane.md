↩ [[HARMONIA]]

A mentor figure who is already embedded in [[Caldwell]] before the war in [[City at War]] begins. Blunt, unimpressed by fear, clearly capable, and just as clearly not in the business of coddling anyone. He teaches the group how to survive, and the group has to decide for itself what that costs.

(Ash and Water has its own, separate Cane.)
