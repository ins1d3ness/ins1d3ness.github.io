↩ [[HARMONIA]]

Not a symbol. A specific, ordinary, slightly irritating and extremely alive thirteen-year-old with opinions about things that don't matter. [[John]]'s feelings for her go unspoken, mostly even to himself; he assumes there will be time to ask the small things later.

Readers should like her for reasons that have nothing to do with anything else in the HLU.
