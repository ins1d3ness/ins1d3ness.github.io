↩ [[HARMONIA]]

The narrator, and the axis the HLU turns on. Glasses, a quick mouth and a quip for every occasion — comedy worn as armor over a grief he rarely names.

At Nocturne Academy, John is the new kid: transferred in mid-year, compared to Harry Potter and to Velma from Scooby-Doo within his first minute, and adopted by [[Danny]] on his first day. Decades later, he's a man who has been away from [[Caldwell]] for far longer than he ever meant to be.

He narrates most of the HLU in first person.
