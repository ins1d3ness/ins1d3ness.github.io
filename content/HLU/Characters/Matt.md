↩ [[HARMONIA]]

Tall, and silent to the point of being mistaken for slow. The class has an entire relationship with a boy who has said perhaps forty words to any of them. He is always around: at the edge of the frame, occasionally somewhere he has no business being, with no explanation offered and none requested.

And then, once in a while, he speaks.
