↩ [[HARMONIA]]

The one who fixes things. Even at thirteen she's the person who produces a bandage from a bag nobody saw her pack. Dry, unsentimental and ferociously kind, she says kind things wrapped in unkind ones, because the kind ones cost too much to say straight.

She is the point-of-view character of [[HARMONIA Resurrection|Resurrection]].
